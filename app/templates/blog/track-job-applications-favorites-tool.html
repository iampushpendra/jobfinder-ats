How to Track Job Applications: Our Free Favorites Tool

October 25, 2026

The average tech job seeker applies to 50–80 roles before landing an offer. The problem isn't the volume — it's the tracking. Which companies did you apply to? Which ones ghosted you? Which roles are still open? Most people use a spreadsheet. We built something simpler.

The Problem With Spreadsheets

Spreadsheets work — until they don't. Here's what actually happens:

  • You find a great role on a job board, copy the link into your sheet, and... the role closes two days later. Your spreadsheet still shows it as "active."
  • You apply to 15 roles in one weekend. By Wednesday, you can't remember which cover letter you sent where.
  • A recruiter emails you: "You applied to our Senior Engineer role — can you remind me which one?" You have no idea which of the three "Senior Engineer" roles they're talking about.
  • You're interviewing at 4 companies simultaneously. You mix up their products in the interview. Game over.

Our Solution: Favorites Built Into the Board

We built a favorites system directly into our job board. Here's how it works — and why it's different:

1. Save Roles With One Click

Every job card on our board has a star button (☆). Click it, and the role is saved to your favorites. No signup. No login. No Google account required. It just works.

2. Live Status Tracking

Because we pull directly from company ATS boards, we know when a role is still open. If a role closes, it disappears from our board — and from your favorites too. No more "dead listings" cluttering your tracker.

3. Email Yourself a Shortlist

Click "Email favorites" in the header and we'll generate a mailto link with all your saved roles formatted as a clean list:

Subject: Job Recommendations from Fortune 500 Companies

Senior Product Manager at Stripe — San Francisco, CA — https://boards.greenhouse.io/stripe/jobs/...
Software Engineer at OpenAI — Remote — https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/openai/...
Data Scientist at Datadog — New York, NY — https://boards.greenhouse.io/datadog/jobs/...

Send it to yourself, your partner, your career coach — anyone who helps you think through decisions.

4. Filter by Favorites

Toggle "Favorites only" in the filter bar to see only your saved roles. Compare them side by side. No more tab-switching between 15 browser tabs.

How to Use It in Your Job Search

Here's a simple workflow that works:

  1. Browse and save. Spend 30 minutes on our board, star every role that looks interesting. Don't overthink it — you can remove stars later.
  2. Review and prioritize. Come back the next day with fresh eyes. Remove the ones that don't feel right. You now have a shortlist.
  3. Apply in batches. Pick 3–5 roles from your shortlist and apply directly through their ATS boards. Use the links in your favorites — they're always current.
  4. Track responses. When you get a response, update your external tracker (Notion, spreadsheet, Trello — whatever you use). Our tool handles the "what's still open" part. You handle the "where am I in the process" part.
  5. Weekly refresh. Check back on our board every week. New roles appear daily. Your favorites auto-update when roles close.

Why It's Free

We're not a job board business. We're a transparency project. Our goal is to make job searching less broken — and one of the biggest broken things is that nobody helps you keep track. So we built it directly into the product.

No signup means no data harvesting. No login means no password to forget. No ads means no distraction. Just a clean job board with a clean tracker.
