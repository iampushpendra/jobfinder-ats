Datadog Jobs: Engineering & Data Science Openings

November 6, 2026

Datadog processes trillions of data points per day. Their platform monitors everything from Kubernetes clusters to serverless functions for 26,000+ customers. Working there means building systems that don't just scale — they scale at a scale most engineers never see.

What Datadog Actually Builds

Datadog's product isn't a single tool — it's an observability platform with a dozen integrated products: infrastructure monitoring, APM, log management, security monitoring, real user monitoring, and more. Each product is a distributed system that ingests, processes, and queries petabytes of data in real time.

This means Datadog engineers work on genuinely hard problems:

  • High-throughput data pipelines. Ingesting millions of events per second with sub-second query latency
  • Distributed query engines. Supporting complex analytics across trillions of data points
  • Auto-scaling infrastructure. Running on AWS, GCP, and Azure simultaneously with zero downtime
  • Security. Detecting threats across customer infrastructure in real time

The Datadog Engineering Bar

Datadog's interview process is rigorous but fair. It typically involves:

  1. Recruiter screen — standard fit conversation
  2. Coding interview — LeetCode medium, often with a systems twist (e.g., "design a rate limiter for a distributed system")
  3. System design — Heavy emphasis on distributed systems: "Design a time-series database" or "How would you shard a metrics ingestion pipeline?"
  4. Domain deep-dive — For SRE roles, expect Kubernetes, Terraform, and incident management questions. For data roles, expect streaming architecture and SQL optimization
  5. Behavioral — Datadog values ownership and operational excellence. Be ready with stories about running production systems

Remote at Datadog

Datadog has offices in New York, Paris, and other hubs, but many roles are remote-friendly within specific regions. The key detail: Datadog is explicit about timezone requirements in their job postings. A "remote" role might require Americas overlap (EST/PST) or EMEA overlap (CET).

This transparency is rare and valuable. You know before applying whether your timezone works.

High-Volume Roles

These functions have the most consistent opening volume at Datadog:

  • Software Engineer — backend, infrastructure, and platform teams
  • Backend Engineer — data ingestion, query engines, and storage systems
  • SRE / Platform Engineer — Kubernetes, Terraform, and observability tooling
  • Data Scientist / Data Engineer — analytics pipelines and ML infrastructure
  • Security Engineer — threat detection and cloud security posture management

Why Datadog Stands Out

Unlike many monitoring companies that bolted on features, Datadog was built as a unified platform from the start. Their engineers don't just write code — they operate it at a scale that forces deep understanding of distributed systems, fault tolerance, and performance optimization.

If you're an engineer who wants to work on genuinely hard infrastructure problems with immediate customer impact, Datadog is one of the best places in tech.
