Best Companies to Work for in 2026 — Live Openings

November 3, 2026

Every year, lists of "best companies to work for" are published by Glassdoor, Fortune, and LinkedIn. They're based on surveys, ratings, and vibes. This list is different — it's based on what actually matters: who's hiring, what they pay, and whether their jobs are real.

Every company on this list has live openings right now. Every link goes directly to their ATS board. No dead listings. No aggregator delay.

The Methodology

We track 23 top tech companies across Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby. This ranking combines:

  • Live opening volume — Companies with the most active roles signal growth and investment
  • Remote flexibility — Companies that hire globally, not just in SF or NYC
  • Compensation transparency — Companies that post salary ranges (legally required in many states now)
  • Engineering reputation — Technical blog quality, open-source contributions, conference presence

Tier 1: The Giants

1. Databricks

The data + AI platform with the highest opening volume on our board. Databricks is hiring aggressively across engineering, product, and sales — and their comp packages are among the highest in data infrastructure. Their live openings consistently top 100+ roles.

2. Stripe

The payments infrastructure company every developer knows. Stripe's hiring bar is notoriously high, but their engineering culture is worth the effort. They maintain steady headcount across backend, infrastructure, and product. Browse their live roles.

3. OpenAI

The most consequential AI company in the world. OpenAI uses Ashby for their ATS and their roles move fast — competitive positions often close within days. Their research and engineering teams are building systems that will define the next decade. See live OpenAI openings.

Tier 2: The High-Growth Challengers

4. Coinbase

Remote-first, public, and hiring steadily across engineering and compliance. Coinbase's security-first engineering culture is a genuine differentiator. Live Coinbase jobs.

5. Datadog

The cloud observability leader. Datadog engineers work on systems that process trillions of events daily — genuine hard problems with immediate customer impact. Browse Datadog openings.

6. Figma

Adobe tried to buy them for $20B and failed. Figma's design tooling is the industry standard, and their engineering team is small but exceptional. Roles are competitive but worth pursuing. Live Figma jobs.

7. Palantir

Not a typical tech company — Palantir embeds engineers inside client organizations. Their Forward Deployed Engineer model is unique, and their stock has been on a historic run. Palantir openings.

Tier 3: The Solid Bets

8. Airbnb

Remote-first with a "work from anywhere" policy. Airbnb's product and design teams are among the best in tech. Their engineering culture emphasizes craft and user obsession. Airbnb jobs.

9. GitLab

The original all-remote company. GitLab's handbook is public, their processes are documented, and their transparency is unmatched. If you want to work remotely with clear expectations, GitLab is the benchmark. GitLab openings.

10. Cloudflare

The internet's infrastructure layer. Cloudflare engineers work on systems that protect and accelerate 20%+ of all web traffic. Their blog and open-source work are legendary. Cloudflare jobs.
