OpenAI Jobs: Where to Find Them and How to Stand Out

November 8, 2026

OpenAI is one of the most selective employers in technology. They're not just hiring engineers — they're hiring researchers, designers, policy specialists, and operations leaders who can operate at the frontier of AI. Getting in requires more than a polished resume.

Where OpenAI Actually Posts Jobs

OpenAI uses Ashby for their ATS. Their official careers page redirects to jobs.ashbyhq.com/openai. Unlike some companies that cross-post everywhere, OpenAI keeps their openings relatively centralized. That means:

  • Their Ashby board is the single source of truth for what's actually open
  • Listings update in real time — when a role closes, it disappears
  • Remote and San Francisco roles dominate, but London and other hubs are growing

We poll OpenAI's Ashby board directly. You can browse all live OpenAI openings here without checking multiple sites.

What OpenAI Looks For

Based on public talks, former employee insights, and job posting patterns, here's what stands out:

Engineering & Research

  • Depth over breadth. OpenAI values people who have gone extremely deep on one hard problem — not generalists who've touched everything.
  • Published or shipped work. GitHub repos with significant stars, papers, or open-source contributions carry real weight.
  • Systems thinking. Many roles involve training runs at unprecedented scale. Experience with distributed systems, GPU clusters, or ML infrastructure is a major plus.

Product & Design

  • AI-native product sense. Can you reason about what users want from an AI system, not just a traditional app?
  • Rapid iteration. OpenAI ships fast. Your portfolio should show evidence of shipping under uncertainty.

Policy & Operations

  • Technical fluency. Even non-technical roles require understanding of how models work, what they can and can't do, and where the risks lie.
  • Stakeholder complexity. Experience navigating regulatory, media, and research stakeholder dynamics is valued.

How to Make Your Application Competitive

  1. Use the direct link. Apply through their Ashby board, not a third-party aggregator. We list live OpenAI jobs with direct apply links.
  2. Reference specific work. In your cover letter or application, reference a specific OpenAI product, paper, or research direction and explain how your work connects.
  3. Show, don't tell. Portfolio links, GitHub repos, and published work matter more than buzzwords on a resume.
  4. Move fast. High-demand roles at OpenAI often close quickly. Set up alerts or check back weekly.

The Interview Process

OpenAI's process is rigorous and typically involves:

  1. Recruiter screen (30 min)
  2. Technical or domain interview (45–60 min)
  3. Take-home or deep-dive session (2–4 hours)
  4. Onsite or virtual onsite (4–5 hours, multiple interviews)
  5. Executive or founder chat (for senior roles)

The technical interviews are known for being problem-driven rather than algorithm trivia. Expect to whiteboard real systems, debug distributed training issues, or design safety mechanisms.

Current OpenAI Roles to Watch

These functions tend to have the most turnover and opening volume:

  • Software Engineer — especially ML infrastructure and research engineering
  • Product Manager — platform and API products
  • Research Scientist — alignment, robotics, multimodal
  • Policy & Safety — growing team as regulatory landscape evolves
