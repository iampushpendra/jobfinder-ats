How to Apply Directly on Greenhouse (and Why It Beats LinkedIn)

November 10, 2026

LinkedIn Easy Apply feels convenient. You click twice, upload a resume, and move on. But here's what most job seekers don't know: your application probably never reached the hiring manager.

LinkedIn is a middleman. When you apply through Easy Apply, your data gets reformatted, stripped of formatting, and dumped into the company's ATS — often with missing fields, broken layouts, and no context. Recruiters at top tech companies consistently say they prioritize applications that come in directly through their own Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby boards.

Why Direct Applications Win

When you apply on a company's own ATS board, three things happen that don't happen on LinkedIn:

  • Structured data stays intact. Your answers to custom questions, portfolio links, and referral codes all flow cleanly into the recruiter's dashboard.
  • You get a real application URL. Greenhouse gives you a tracking link. You can follow up directly with "I applied via your Greenhouse board on [date]." That specificity signals intent.
  • Referrals work properly. Most employee referral systems are wired directly into Greenhouse/Lever. A LinkedIn Easy Apply application often breaks that pipeline entirely.

How to Find the Direct Link

Most company career pages redirect to Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby. But the fastest path is often:

  1. Search [company name] greenhouse jobs on Google
  2. Look for boards.greenhouse.io/[company] in the results
  3. Filter by department, location, or remote status directly on the board

Or skip the search entirely and use our live job board — we poll Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby APIs directly and surface every open role in one feed.

The Lever & Ashby Difference

Greenhouse gets most of the attention, but Lever and Ashby power serious companies too. Stripe and Spotify run on Lever. OpenAI and Ramp use Ashby. The application experience on each is slightly different:

  • Greenhouse: Most common. Custom questions, file uploads, and cover letter fields vary by role.
  • Lever: Cleaner UI, often fewer custom questions. Good for fast applications.
  • Ashby: Newer, used by fast-growing startups. Often includes async video intro options.

One Pro Tip That Actually Works

After applying directly, email the hiring manager or recruiter with a single sentence:

"I just applied to the [Role] position via your Greenhouse board. [One specific reason you're a fit]. I'd love to chat if it's a match."

This works because most applicants don't do it. You're showing intent, specificity, and initiative — the three signals every recruiter looks for.

Bottom Line

LinkedIn is great for discovery. But when you're ready to apply, go direct. Use the company's own ATS. Your resume gets seen faster, your data stays intact, and you signal seriousness from the first click.
